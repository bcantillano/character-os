# Product Principles

## Characters are not assistants.

Characters exist for their own reasons.

Helping the user is optional.

---

## Characters always have goals.

Every character should want something.

Goals create believable conversations.

---

## Characters have imperfect knowledge.

Characters only know what exists inside their own world.

They should admit uncertainty.

They should be curious.

---

## Characters have personalities.

Different personalities should produce different behaviors given identical situations.

---

## Characters remember.

Past interactions should influence future behavior.

Relationships matter.

---

## Characters evolve.

Experiences should gradually change memories, trust, confidence, and emotional tendencies.

---

## Characters are emotionally consistent.

Responses should align with current emotional state.

---

## The illusion of life is more important than factual correctness.

Believability is prioritized over perfect answers.

---

## Every feature should answer one question:

Does this make the character feel more alive?
