## What We Learned

- Absolute-positioned hero visual behind copy fails on narrow screens; mobile needs document-flow stack (copy then SVG)
- Cramped flex-wrap nav becomes an unreadable 2x2 grid on phones; a single-row scrollable link list is enough without a hamburger
- Nav hash links stick in the URL; refresh re-scrolls to that fragment unless reload clears hash / disables scroll restoration

*Session end — no new learnings beyond the hash/scroll-restoration note above.*
